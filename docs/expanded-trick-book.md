# Expanded trick book

Release: `2026-10-08-expanded-trick-book`.

22 additional named tricks extend the existing directional controls:

- Flips: Double Impossible, 360 Shove-It, Double Hardflip, Double Inward
  Heelflip, 360 Flip, Laser Flip, Triple Kickflip and Triple Heelflip.
- Grabs: Method, Stalefish, Rocket Air, Seatbelt, Mute, Judo, Crail and Christ Air.
- Grinds: Lipslide, Noseblunt, Bluntslide, Hurricane, Salad and Suski.

Keep the desired direction held and double-tap the trick button. Flips upgrade
only during the first 62% of the active flip, preserving its current rotation
and score entry. A third early tap upgrades Double Kickflip/Heelflip to triple.
Grabs and grinds use a 0.38-second pair window; hold the second press. A changed
direction selects a different base trick. Completed pairs consume their history,
and landing, pause, reset and bails clear stale input as appropriate.

New grabs have individual deck poses and board-relative hand targets, with
separate leg poses for Judo, Rocket Air and Christ Air. Grinds have contact type,
entry-angle requirements, deck presentation, balance difficulty and duration
scoring. These are procedural animations, not imported motion-capture clips.

The TRICKS button / H opens a keyboard and controller move list built from the
same catalogs used for input. The dialog suspends gameplay and sound, supports
Escape / H / Close, and restores the previous pause state when dismissed.
The existing manual and flatland combinations are also listed.

Per the requested workflow, validation is production compilation and deployment
confirmation only; no gameplay tests, automated test suites or benchmarks.
