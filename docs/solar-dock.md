# Solar Dock

The default park is an original desert skate terminal, generated by
`src/park/SolarDockPark.js`. The older imported park remains at `/?park=legacy`.
The default route does not download any of the old park GLBs.

## Layout

The plaza is 108 × 148 metres, Y-up, with four connected skating zones:

- Street: bank tabletop, four-sided pyramid, manual pad, long ledge, flat rails,
  six stairs with a down rail and an adjacent bank.
- Orbit: circular pool, 28-metre diameter, 3.8-metre depth, continuous coping.
- Flow: two 3.8-metre quarter transitions sharing a 10-metre flat bottom.
- Mega: 11-metre roll-in, 3.3-metre kicker, 7-metre gap, broad landing and a
  separate 4.5-metre return quarter. A side bank reaches the start platform.

A 5-metre vert wall anchors the north end. The area buttons reset at Street,
Pool, Half-pipe or Mega while keeping the session score. R subsequently resets
at the selected area. Changing areas clears the current trick/combo.

## Geometry and collision

Riding geometry and its collision proxy share vertices and triangle winding.
The plaza has an actual pool opening; no ground plane covers the bowl. The
background desert floor sits below the deepest skating surface.

Quarter decks extend the same profile instead of putting a hidden wall at the
lip. Side/end walls use outward winding and solid collision. Grind rail/post
proxies retain rail IDs for selective contact exclusion during a grind. Coping
has grind metadata but no rigid capsule obstructing vert launches. Painted
markings and scenery outside the boundary have no collision.

The new coping carries inline transition metadata consumed by the existing
TransitionController. The mega kicker is deliberately not classified as vert.
Rendering is batched by material; rideable collision is merged for BVH queries.
No imported textures or newly downloaded assets are needed for the park.

## Camera

High Follow is the default: 9.5 metres behind, 5.4 metres above the rider anchor
on the ground, with extra elevation in the air. It tracks travel rather than
trick rotation, holds the ramp side during vert return, and retains occlusion
clearance. C / R3 cycles High Follow → Classic → Fixed. Preferences use the
new `streetskate.cameraMode.v2` key to retire the earlier fixed-view default.

## Release scope

Only the production build is run for this change, following the user's request
to leave gameplay testing and benchmarks to them. Runtime feel, gap tuning and
edge cases remain subject to their playthrough; compilation is not gameplay
validation.
